Our Farm

Wagyu Farming in the Hallertau

A small, personal farm with a clear philosophy – calm, grounded and fully focused on animal welfare and quality.

About Us

On our farm in the Hallertau, we dedicate ourselves with great care to the husbandry and breeding of Wagyu cattle. Our priorities are animal welfare, a calm approach with the animals and sustainable farm management.

Our animals grow up in a close-to-nature environment with pasture access and low-stress rearing. We place great importance on responsible farming and a conscious approach to quality.

With Hallertau Wagyu we want to offer an honest and transparent insight into our operation – for everyone who wants to learn more about our work.

01

Pasture & Nature

Our animals have access to pastures and grow up in a calm, natural environment – without unnecessary stress and with plenty of space.

02

Hallertau Region

The Hallertau, with its meadows and tranquil landscape, offers ideal conditions for the low-stress rearing of Wagyu cattle.

Sustainable

We farm with an eye to the future: healthy animals, well-managed land and an honest, transparent operation.
