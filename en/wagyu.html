The Breed

Wagyu – Quality & Care

Wagyu is renowned worldwide for its fine marbling and exceptional beef quality. The foundation: good genetics, time and daily attentiveness.

What Makes Wagyu Special

Wagyu cattle are characterised by a unique intramuscular fat distribution, which gives the meat its distinctive marbling. This fat has a particularly high proportion of unsaturated fatty acids and results in a tender, aromatic flavour experience.

For us, it is not just the end product that matters, but above all the responsible rearing and respectful treatment of the animals. Quality comes from many factors: healthy animals, appropriate husbandry conditions, patience and daily care.
