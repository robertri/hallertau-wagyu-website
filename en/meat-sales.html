Meat Sales

Wagyu Beef from the Hallertau

From our pastures directly to your kitchen – our Wagyu beef is produced in small quantities, under careful conditions, and with a standard we uphold every day.

Introduction

Beef with Provenance and Purpose

Every animal that leaves our farm has been raised with care – on extensive pastures in the Hallertau, following the rhythm of the seasons. Our aim is not maximum production, but Wagyu beef of a quality that this exceptional breed deserves.

Those who purchase our beef know where it comes from. That matters to us.

Current Status

The Beef is Now Ageing

This morning, our first Wagyu bull was harvested on the pasture and taken directly to the butcher. The meat is now being carefully processed and dry-aged.

The pasture harvest is the most animal-friendly method of slaughter: the animal experiences no stress from transport or abattoir handling, and is taken without warning in its natural environment. This also has a measurable positive effect on meat quality.

Ageing is expected to be complete by 24 April 2026. Premium cuts such as fillet and sirloin will hang for longer, as confirmed by our butcher – who assessed the quality as excellent.

The final sales structure is currently being defined. If you are interested, we encourage you to get in touch now – we will notify you as soon as the beef is ready for collection.
