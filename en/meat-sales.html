Meat Sales

Wagyu Beef from the Hallertau

From our pastures directly to your kitchen – our Wagyu beef is produced in small quantities, under careful conditions, and with a standard we uphold every day.

Introduction

Beef with Provenance and Purpose

Every animal that leaves our farm has been raised with care – on extensive pastures in the Hallertau, following the rhythm of the seasons. Our aim is not maximum production, but Wagyu beef of a quality that this exceptional breed deserves.

Those who purchase our beef know where it comes from. That matters to us.

Current Status

First Sale in Preparation

Process

From Enquiry to Collection

01

Enquiry

Send us an email or give us a call. We will discuss availability, possible cuts and quantities together.

02

Coordination

We discuss the form of purchase with you – whether individual cuts, larger quantities or specific requests – and clarify all details personally.

03

Collection

Collection takes place directly at our farm. On request, we are happy to share more about the origin and husbandry of your animal.

What We Offer

Our Offer

Wagyu beef can be purchased in several ways – as a whole or half animal, as selected cuts, or in agreed quantities. We work with a regional butcher who processes the meat carefully according to our requirements.

We do not offer standardised packages. Instead, we speak with you directly about what makes sense for you – and what our current production allows.

Note

Limited Production – by Choice

We produce Wagyu beef in small quantities. This is not a limitation, but a decision. Only in this way can we guarantee the quality that Wagyu stands for.

If you have a genuine interest, we recommend getting in touch early. This allows us to take your interest into account when the next availability arises.
