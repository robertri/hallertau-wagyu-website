Our Farm

We run a Wagyu cow-calf operation in the Hallertau region of Bavaria. Our priorities are animal welfare, low-stress husbandry and a close-to-nature approach.

Hallertau Wagyu stands for a small, personal farm with a clear philosophy – calm, grounded and focused on what truly matters.
